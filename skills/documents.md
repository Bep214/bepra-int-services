# Documents and reports

Official Anthropic skills. These are built and maintained by Anthropic and already switched on for anyone on a paid Claude plan. Nothing to install. You likely have these right now.

---

## 1. PowerPoint (pptx)

**What it is:** Builds and edits real .pptx slide decks, not screenshots of slides.

**Business use:** Pitch decks, client presentations, quarterly updates in minutes.

**How to use it:** Ask Claude to create a presentation on your topic. It produces a real PowerPoint file you can open, edit, and present.

**Link:** https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/pptx

---

## 2. Excel (xlsx)

**What it is:** Creates and edits real spreadsheets with working formulas and charts.

**Business use:** Quotes, invoices, cashflow, simple financial models.

**How to use it:** Describe the spreadsheet you need. Claude builds it with live formulas, not a flat table.

**Link:** https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/xlsx

---

## 3. Word (docx)

**What it is:** Produces formatted Word documents with headings, tables, and letterheads.

**Business use:** Proposals, contracts, letters, policies that look professional.

**How to use it:** Tell Claude what document you need and the format. It produces a clean, formatted Word file.

**Link:** https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/docx

---

## 4. PDF

**What it is:** Fills forms, merges, splits, and pulls text or tables out of PDFs.

**Business use:** Fill supplier forms, combine quotes, extract data from invoices.

**How to use it:** Give Claude a PDF and tell it what you need done — fill it, split it, or pull the data out.

**Link:** https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/pdf
